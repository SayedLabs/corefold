date: 2026-10-21

## Corefold 1.5.1: bug fixes

### Pictures
- iPhone photos keep their colours and the date they were taken after converting, resizing, rotating or watermarking.
- 16-bit pictures (from scanners and some cameras) no longer come out white.
- Making a PDF from lots of pictures, or using Scan, no longer runs out of memory.
- "Convert to PNG" no longer copies files that are already PNG.

### Video and documents
- Phone videos converted to MKV are no longer sideways, and iPhone MOV files now convert to MKV.
- Converting to WebM is much faster.
- Converting to PDF with PowerPoint, Word or Excel no longer closes the documents you have open.
- Converting with LibreOffice now works while LibreOffice is open.
- Compressing a PDF no longer puts white boxes around see-through pictures.

### Archives
- "Extract into its own folder" no longer leaves the folder hidden.
- A failed archive can no longer delete other files with a similar name.
- After you type the right password, files that already worked aren't extracted twice.
- Big archives no longer freeze the window before extracting or opening.
- The same archive no longer opens in two windows, and search shows what's inside matching folders.

### Everything else
- Two conversions at once can no longer save over each other.
- Files named CON, NUL and similar can now be saved.
- Right-clicking hundreds of files at once no longer skips some of them.
- "Convert to JPG?" no longer pops up for pictures Corefold made itself.
- Moving the portable folder no longer breaks "Start with Windows".
- The watermark and password windows work better, and the wheel remembers its page.
- Settings and output folders are written to less often.
- Updates show a clearer message if the internet drops, and old update files are cleaned up.
