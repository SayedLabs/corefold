date: 2026-10-25

Files first
- This version puts everything into the Files tools: Converter, PDF, Archive, File Organizer, Unlock and Cloud.
- Images & screen, Computer and Quick tools are resting while they're made better. Their pages show what's coming back. Nothing of them runs in the background, and your settings and saved things in them are kept for when they return.

Home
- Add widgets to the Home page: quick actions for each Files tool, and shortcuts to your folders, apps, websites and Corefold pages.
- Pick each widget's size (small, medium, large or wide), drag it where you want it, or remove it. Press Customize on the Home page to start.

Updates
- Before you update, the Updates page shows the new version's day and what's in it.
- After an update, "What's new" opens once, and the Version history on the Updates page has every version's notes, even offline.
- An update keeps Corefold in the folder it's in (a folder you picked too), keeps your desktop icon and start-with-Windows choice as they were, and a pinned taskbar icon stays one icon.
- An update cut off half-way (a power cut, say) leaves your Corefold as it was, never half old and half new.
- If an update didn't finish, Corefold says so the next time it starts, with a button to try again.

PDF
- Pages deleted in Edit content are really gone from the saved file, and changes count as saved only once the file was written.
- What you cover in Edit content is taken out of the file, not just hidden.
- Arrange, Merge and Split keep forms and bookmarks. A locked PDF keeps its password in Compress, Split and Merge.
- Page numbers, watermarks and signatures land inside the part of the page you see.
- A PDF of thousands of pages, or 40 PDFs dropped at once, shows its pages in seconds, and turning, deleting and Undo stay instant.

Converter and Archive
- Multi-page TIFFs and animated pictures keep all their pages and frames, and colour profiles are kept.
- Converting a folder never writes over a file that has the same name.
- Cancel on an Office to PDF conversion stops at once, and nothing half-made is left behind.
- Subtitles in Spanish or French come out right, and Outlook emails with emoji open.
- Cancelling a new ZIP next to an old split archive no longer deletes the old archive's parts.
- Links inside an archive (which can point anywhere on the PC) are left out when unpacking.
- Dragging files out of an open archive no longer makes the window stop answering.
- Archives of hundreds of thousands of files stay smooth: searching, sorting and folders of 100,000 files never freeze the window.
- Hundreds of big photos at once, or one picture of hundreds of megapixels, are converted without running short of memory.

Cloud, File Organizer and Unlock
- Copying a folder into itself stops at once. An account you signed out of stays signed out, and being offline is shown as offline.
- Edits to a file you opened from an online account are kept.
- Files you open from Cloud are kept in Corefold's own folder, where Windows' clean-up and uninstalling Corefold leave them alone. A file you saved under another name next to one of them is kept too.
- A download never lands on top of a file that took its name meanwhile.
- If the list of your cloud accounts can't be read, it's put aside, never written over.
- Cloud's menus are clearer: icons, the keys to press, and Open in File Explorer for each cloud folder (and Remove from the list for one you added).
- The daily tidy and a sort you start yourself never run at the same time.
- Unlock never closes a part of Windows.
- A cloud folder of 20,000 pictures opens and sorts at once.
- File Organizer stays smooth with tens of thousands of files: leaving a whole kind out is instant, and Undo of a huge sort takes seconds.

Steadier
- Quitting always works, and asks first only about unsaved work.
- If the settings file can't be read for a moment, it's never written over with the defaults. A damaged one comes back from the copy made before the last update.
