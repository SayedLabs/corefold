Corefold 1.7.5

New: the Store
- Choose the tools you want. A tool you remove isn't loaded at all, so Corefold stays light.
- On the first start, Corefold asks which tools you want.
- After this update, the new tools wait in the Store. Nothing new is switched on for you.

New: Corefold Viewer
- Flip through a folder of pictures: zoom, turn, crop and save.
- Open Outlook (.msg) and .eml emails without Outlook, with their attachments and Save as PDF. Pictures from the internet are taken out.
- Pictures and emails can open with a double-click.

New: Corefold Player
- Videos and music, then the rest of the folder.
- Subtitles from the video or from a file, shifted in time when they're early or late.
- Speed, A-B loop and clips, frame by frame, save a frame.
- Mini player, full screen, and Continue watching from where you stopped.

New: Corefold Clipboard
- Everything you copy is kept on this PC: text, links, pictures and files.
- Win + Alt + V opens it in any app and pastes what you pick. Search, pins and limits.
- Copies that password managers mark as private are never kept.

New: Corefold Space
- A map of what fills a drive, down into the folders inside, with a coloured legend.
- Look through several folders at once, clean up safely, and find exact duplicates.

New: Corefold Capture
- Screenshots (Ctrl + Print Screen) with an editor: arrows, boxes, marker, text, blur, crop.
- Screen recording (Ctrl + Shift + Print Screen).

Fixes
- Arabic: sentences that start with an English word or a number read in the right order.
- Done and the note about saving only show on the settings pages.
- Renaming a single file shows its name, and the watermark logo buttons have room between them.

Coming soon
- Corefold Cloud: OneDrive and Dropbox side by side.

Your settings, history and backups are kept.
