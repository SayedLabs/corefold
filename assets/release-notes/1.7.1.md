# Corefold 1.7.1: your tools, all in one place

## 🧰 Corefold is now a set of tools
- A new **Home** page: every tool as a card, what's coming next, and your recent activity
- **Corefold Converter** has its own page, with its on/off switch, how-to and drop zone
- **Corefold Uninstaller** (the old Apps page) has its own name and look
- A **Tools** section in the sidebar, with Archive, Player and Viewer coming soon

## 🔔 Softer sounds
- Every sound was remade to be warmer and quieter: Pebble (new), Droplet, Glass, the problem sound and the wheel tick

## ✅ Checked tool by tool
- Every option on the wheel was run on real files on Windows: pictures, video, music, PDFs, archives and folders
- Taking the text out of a scanned PDF now says there's no text, instead of making an empty file
- The PDF pages window copes with a PDF that changed after it was opened
- Tool cards keep long lines (like Arabic) inside the card, and work with the keyboard

Your settings, history and backups are kept.
