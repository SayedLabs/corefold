New
- PDF: the editor can now change everything on a page: text inside groups, turned text, drawn shapes, the PDF's own comments, notes and links. Words in fonts that can't be read, and scanned pages, are read first so they can be changed too.
- PDF: turn a PDF into a Word document with the Word on your PC.
- PDF: compare two PDFs (changed pages side by side, and every word that changed), and print a PDF from inside the window.
- Converter: choose H.265 or AV1 for videos, save pictures as JPEG XL, and make PNGs smaller without changing a single dot.
- Space: a much faster look at whole drives (Windows asks for permission), and clean-up by itself every week or month (only temporary files and caches of closed apps).
- Space: turn off the hibernation file, remove older restore points (the newest is kept), and compress Windows (Compact OS). None of these is ticked by itself.
- Cloud: add your own NAS or server (WebDAV, SFTP, FTP, network folders).
- Cloud: back up a folder into any account, by itself every day or week. Nothing is ever deleted in the account.
- Uninstaller: open a leftover folder or registry key and untick what should stay; only the rest goes, with the same backup and undo.
- Uninstaller: watch an app's install, so everything it put in is offered for removal with it later.
- Uninstaller: stop programs forcing extensions into your browsers.
