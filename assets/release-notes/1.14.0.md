New
- PDF: Remove hidden info. See and change a PDF's title and author, and take out who made it, notes, attached files, scripts and other hidden details before you send it.
- Tidy up: your own rules. Send files with certain words in their name, or of certain types, to the folder you choose. Files can also go into a folder for each year (and month); photos go by the day they were taken.
- Cloud: Copy a share link. Get a link anyone can open (see only) for a file on Google Drive, OneDrive or Dropbox.
- Convert: presets. Keep your own conversions with their own quality and size (like WhatsApp, YouTube 1080p or Photos for email); they show on the wheel and in the right-click menu. Sound quality for MP3, M4A, OGG and Opus can be set too.
- Unlock: USB drives. Windows says the drive is in use? See which program is using it, close it, and eject the drive safely in one go.
- Convert: more picture formats. Cameras' RAW photos (CR2, CR3, NEF, ARW, DNG and more) and drawings (SVG) can be converted, and pictures can be saved as TIFF and GIF.
