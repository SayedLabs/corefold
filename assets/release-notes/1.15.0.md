date: 2026-10-27

New
- Convert keeps what's inside a file: a video keeps all its sound tracks and the subtitles the new kind of file can hold (text subtitles in MP4, MOV and WebM; every kind in MKV), and a song keeps its album cover in MP3, M4A and FLAC.
- Unlock finds programs that are using a folder, like a Command Prompt opened in it or a program started from inside it, so closing them frees the folder.
- PDF editor: a line you change keeps its font. Corefold looks for the PDF's font on your PC and among ten free fonts that now come with it (Cairo, Tajawal, Almarai, IBM Plex Sans Arabic, El Messiri, Markazi Text, Roboto, Open Sans, Montserrat and Poppins). On a line with Arabic and English, each keeps its own font. The font list shows every font on your PC that writes English or Arabic.
- Files shows everything it can do with a file you drop: a PDF also gets Edit content, Arrange pages, Make searchable and more, and anything can be asked "What's using this?".
- PDFs dropped together with pictures can become one PDF, in the order they came.

Fixes
- Turning on "Install updates by themselves" now updates Corefold right away when a new version is waiting, instead of at the next check up to 6 hours later. Turning automatic checks back on checks at once.
- When Corefold starts with Windows before the internet is ready, it checks for updates again after a few minutes, instead of 6 hours later.
- Unpacking an archive that holds two files with the same name keeps both, instead of one replacing the other.
- Making a picture smaller for sharing keeps its see-through parts (a logo or a sticker stays a PNG) instead of turning them white.
- Stars, arrows and other small marks that Windows' font can't show now look right everywhere.
- Archive: .bz2, .gz, .xz and .lzma files show what's inside, and big ones no longer stop as a false "archive bomb".
- Unlock no longer offers to close every program when you pick a whole drive or a Windows folder, and Rename now asks inside the main window.
- Section names in French, Spanish and Turkish are no longer cut off in the sidebar, and Settings shows its list at the usual window size.
- The Pause item by the clock now says "Pause the Converter".
- Setup and uninstall also speak Arabic, French, Spanish and Turkish.

Safer
- Cloud sign-in keys never appear in Corefold's log or in a message.
- Installing, updating or removing Corefold only closes Corefold's own copy. Another program that happens to have the same name (or Corefold's old name), like a portable Corefold, keeps running.
- File Organizer is resting for now while it's checked again, so no files are moved by mistake. It comes back in a later update, with your settings and Undo kept.
- Archive: an archive that hides its real size stops at a thousand times its size instead of filling the disk. Items that would land outside the folder are left out, links never come out, and names Windows keeps for itself (like CON) are saved safely.
- Archive comes with the newest 7-Zip (26.05), which has security fixes.
- PDF Cover also removes the hidden text under it (in searchable scans) and the page's small preview picture.
- Pictures to PDF never puts a photo's camera details (GPS place, phone, dates) into the PDF.
- PDF Remove hidden info also removes scripts in bookmarks and forms, actions that open web pages or other files by themselves, and the details inside photos.
- Compress, Page numbers, Watermark and Sign keep a locked PDF locked.
- A problem report leaves out your quick links, watermark words, ignored programs and picked colours.
- Importing settings warns you when they would save files to a folder on another computer.

Easier to read
- Grey text (second lines and small notes) is darker and easier to read in both looks, with every accent colour.
- Every setting has a name a screen reader can say.
- The Archive window gives more room to file names when it's narrow.
- The Arabic is now clear, standard Arabic everywhere, with the same words for the same things in every tool, and counts read right (ملفان, 3 ملفات, 11 ملفًا).

Smoother
- The Updates page opens at once (in Arabic it could take up to 3 seconds).
- Switching between the dark and light look is quicker.
