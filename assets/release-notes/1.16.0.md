New
- Space and the Uninstaller are back, in the Computer section. Space shows what's filling each drive and frees up space safely; the Uninstaller removes programs and what they leave behind, with a backup you can put back. Both are switched on for everyone, and you can switch either off in Computer if you don't need it. Startup and Virus scan rest a little longer.

Safer
- Space's clean-up looks again right before it removes anything: a temporary file used again since it was found stays, and a browser's or app's cache stays if the browser or app was opened meanwhile.
- Your own Windows folders (Desktop, Documents, Downloads, Pictures, OneDrive and the like) can't be sent to the Recycle Bin from Space's map. What's inside them still can.
