Corefold 1.7.6

A whole new look
- A new design from top to bottom: window frame, start page, Store, History, About and Settings.
- Home shows your tools in two columns, so everything fits without scrolling.
- The sidebar is yours: drag tools into the order you like, make it wider or narrower, or switch to icons only with the ☰ button. Small windows show icons only on their own.
- Space shows your drives as cards with how full each one is. The bar turns amber at 80% and red at 90%.

Find anything
- Ctrl + K finds any page, setting or action. Just start typing.

Quick access from any app
- Ctrl + Alt + Space opens a quick menu over whatever you're doing.
- Hold Alt + ` for a quick wheel: point at a button and let go. Choose its buttons and shortcuts in Settings.
- Optional: a small floating button that opens the wheel.

Going back
- Alt + Left, your mouse's back button and Esc take you back, everywhere.
- In Space: up a folder, then back to your drives. Going back while it's still looking stops it.

Fixes
- Space opens whole drives like C:, even with over a million files.
- Adding or removing a tool no longer shows a white window for a moment, and the Store keeps its place.
- Esc in the clipboard popup hands the keyboard back to where you were typing.
- Small windows: Settings scroll, and no buttons or text get cut off.
- Turning "open files with Corefold" on and off quickly works every time.
- Messages like "copied" or "cleaned up" no longer play the problem sound.

Coming soon
- Corefold Cloud, Startup, PDF, Rename and Search, plus more ideas in the Store.

Your settings, history and backups are kept.
