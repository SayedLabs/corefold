Corefold 1.5.0

New
• Arabic (العربية). Corefold follows your Windows language; change it in General → Language.
• Right-click menu like a normal Windows app: right-click → Corefold → Convert to PNG, Resize…, Extract here… (Prefer the wheel? General → Right-click menu.)
• Much lighter on your PC: conversions run in the background and one at a time, Corefold gives memory back when it's idle, and the download is smaller.
• "Stop all conversions" in the tray menu, and memory use shown on the About page.

Fixed
• No more freezing when dropping big videos or PDFs
• Right-clicking many files at once is fast and reliable
• Updates and restarts no longer get stuck
• Settings and history can't be damaged if the PC turns off while saving
• Plus several smaller fixes
