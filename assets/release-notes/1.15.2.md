Smoother
- Opening a huge archive (like 100,000 files in one folder) no longer stops the window for a moment when it appears.
- Searching inside a huge archive no longer stops the window: the rows that don't match are hidden a part at a time.
- The main window opens faster: the Home page shows its first widgets at once and the rest right after.
- Starting a job on files from a slow drive no longer stops the window: their sizes are read in the background.
- Closing an Archive or picture window now frees its memory right away (a huge archive's list stayed in memory until the next one was opened, and made the app stop for a moment now and then).
- Switching a tool on or off in Settings no longer stops the window for a moment: the Home page is made again much faster.
