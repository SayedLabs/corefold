date: 2026-10-24

Tools in four sections
- The sidebar now has four sections: Files, Images & screen, Computer, and Quick tools. Each section shows its tools with an on/off switch (this replaces the Store). A tool that's switched off loads nothing.
- A strip above each tool's page moves you between the tools of its section. Ctrl + K still finds every tool.

New in Corefold PDF: Edit content
- Type text anywhere, and change the PDF's own lines (Arabic too, right to left).
- Add, move and resize pictures, including the ones already in the PDF.
- Highlight, underline and cross out words, draw by hand, and add rectangles, circles, lines and arrows.
- Cover really removes what's under it from the file, not just hides it.
- Notes, signatures and filling in forms. Undo and redo.
- Always saved as a new PDF. A locked PDF keeps its password.

Corefold Cloud is ready
- Works with OneDrive, Dropbox, Google Drive and iCloud through the folders their own apps keep on your PC. No sign-in needed.
- Whole folders, a queue, and search across all your accounts.

Capture
- Select text: drag over the words in a screenshot and copy just those (Arabic and English).
- Choose the folder your screenshots are saved in.
- Recording videos and GIFs is taken out for now. It comes back later as an update of its own.

More in every tool
- Archives: "Extract to…" lets you pick a folder, from the wheel and the right-click menu.
- Viewer: Open with… and Print…. Player and files inside an archive: Open with….
- Converter: keep the original's date, or send the original to the Recycle Bin once it's converted (both off to begin with).
