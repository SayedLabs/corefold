# Corefold 1.6.0: the biggest update yet

## 🧹 Uninstall apps, without the leftovers
A new **Apps** page lists every program and Store app on your PC, with its size and install date.

- **Uninstall** runs the app's own uninstaller first, then finds what it left behind: folders, shortcuts, startup entries and registry keys
- Things that clearly belong to the app are ticked; anything that only *might* is shown unticked, so you decide
- Windows, Microsoft and shared folders are never touched
- **Nothing is deleted for good.** Everything removed is kept as a backup for 30 days, and one click puts it all back
- Uninstall several apps at once, quietly
- Make a System Restore point first if you want
- "Remove leftovers only" for apps whose uninstaller is missing or broken

## ✨ New tools
**Pictures**
- **Crop** with a box you drag: free, square, 4:3, 16:9 or phone size
- **Stickers** for WhatsApp and Telegram (animated GIFs stay animated)

**Video & audio**
- **Speed up or slow down** (from 0.25× to 4×; voices keep their normal pitch)
- **Mute** a video
- **Fix the volume** of recordings that are too quiet or too loud
- **Animated stickers** and chat GIFs for WhatsApp, Telegram and Discord

**PDFs**
- **Pages:** turn, delete and reorder pages, with thumbnails you can drag
- **Pictures:** save every picture inside a PDF
- **Lock / Unlock:** add or remove a password (AES-256)

**Files & folders**
- **Rename** many files at once: numbers, find & replace, or add text, with **Undo**
- **Folders:** convert every picture inside to JPG, PNG or WebP, or shrink them all for sharing

## ⚡ Faster to use
- **Do it again:** repeat your last action, with the same choices, on other files (Home page and tray menu)
- **Compare:** after converting a picture, slide between the original and the new one
- **Number keys:** when the wheel opens from the right-click menu, press 1–9 to pick an option
- **Right-click any file**, of any type, to zip, 7z or rename it
- A short welcome tour for new users

## 🎨 Make it yours
- **Three new wheel looks:** Bubbles, Neon and Glass
- **Rainbow** slice colours
- Choose the text size in Corefold's windows
- Choose which corner popups appear in and how long they stay
- Turn the sidebar icons on or off

## 🌍 More languages
Corefold now speaks **French, Spanish and Turkish**, alongside English and Arabic. It follows your Windows language automatically, or you can pick one in Settings.

## 🛠️ Fixes
- Renaming puts everything back if something goes wrong halfway
- Numbered files are sorted the way File Explorer sorts them (2 before 10)
- Huge photos open in the crop and compare windows without using lots of memory
- Long option names on the wheel are easier to read
- Lots of smaller fixes and polish throughout

---
**Updating:** Corefold updates itself. Click **Update now** when it asks, or check from the **Updates** page. All your settings are kept.
