## Corefold 1.4.0 – the big one

### 🗜️ Archives (ZIP, RAR, 7z and more)
- Open and extract ZIP, RAR, 7z, ISO, TAR, GZ, CAB and more, no extra apps needed
- Smart extract: no more folder-inside-a-folder
- Look inside an archive, search it, open or take out single files
- Make ZIP or 7z files with a password, max compression, or split into parts
- Test archives for damage, and turn RAR into ZIP or 7z
- New right-click options: "Open in Corefold" and "Compress with Corefold"

### ✨ New tools
- **Fit size**: shrink videos, pictures, PDFs and music to fit Discord, email and more
- **Copy text**: grab the text from a screenshot or photo
- **Watermark** your pictures and videos (with a live preview)
- Save pictures from a video, or turn pictures into a video
- **Scan**: turn photos of paper into a clean PDF
- **Paste**: convert a copied picture or copied files (Ctrl + V)
- Downloaded an iPhone photo (HEIC) or WebP? Corefold offers a JPG copy

### 🎡 A smarter wheel
- See how many options you have, and dots for each page
- "More" slice when there are lots of options (click it, or hold the file over it)
- New look options: huge size, icons, text size, colors by type, options per page, and more
- Reorder options any way you like

### 🏠 New Home page
Drop files on Corefold's window to see every option as buttons, grouped and explained.

### 🔊 New sounds
Four soft new sounds made for Corefold, a volume slider, and a gentle sound when something goes wrong.
