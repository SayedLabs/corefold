Corefold 1.8.0

New: Corefold PDF
- Merge PDFs and arrange their pages by dragging the page pictures. Turn and delete pages, and undo any step.
- Split a PDF, or take out just the pages you need.
- Make a big PDF small enough to send, in three levels.
- Pictures to PDF, on A4 or Letter if you want, without compressing them again. PDF pages to pictures.
- Lock a PDF with a password, with or without printing and copying, or unlock one that's yours.
- Sign a PDF: draw your signature, or use a picture of it.
- Number the pages, and add a text or logo watermark to every page, with a preview first.
- Everything happens on your PC. Your PDFs are never changed; the result is saved as a new file.

New: Corefold File Organizer
- Sorts Downloads, the Desktop or any folder into Pictures, Documents, Videos, Music, Archives, Installers and Fonts with one click. Add it from the Store.
- You see where every file goes first, and Undo puts everything back.
- Folders, shortcuts and files that are open are left alone.

New: Corefold Startup
- Every program that starts with Windows, in one list. Add it from the Store.
- Switch one off with a click, and back on just as easily. It works the same way as Task Manager, so nothing is ever deleted.
- Programs that added themselves since you last looked are marked.

New: Corefold Scan
- Check your PC for malware with Microsoft Defender, part of Windows. Nothing is uploaded. Add it from the Store.
- Quick scan, full scan, or right-click any file, folder or drive.
- Spots tricks viruses use: fake names like photo.jpg.exe, broken signatures, and odd programs that start with Windows.
- See what Defender put in quarantine, and put a file back if you're sure.

New: Corefold Awake
- Keeps your PC from going to sleep: until you turn it off, for a while, or until a time. Keep the screen on too if you want.
- A shut-down timer that turns the PC off at the time you pick, even if Corefold is closed by then.
- Switch it on and off from the tray.

New: Corefold Colors
- Win + Shift + C picks any colour on your screen, with a magnifier that shows every pixel.
- Click to copy it as HEX, RGB or HSL. Your last 24 colours are kept, ready to copy again.

New: Corefold Unlock
- Right-click a file or folder you can't delete or move and pick "What's using this?".
- Corefold asks that program to close first, so it can save your work, then lets you delete or move the file.
- It never closes the parts of Windows your PC needs.

Quick menu (Ctrl + Alt + Space)
- Type a sum or a unit conversion and get the answer. Enter copies it.
- Find apps and files as you type, and open Windows Settings pages by name.
- Sleep, Restart, Shut down, Sign out and Empty the Recycle Bin. The risky ones ask first.
- Search the web from the last row. Nothing is sent unless you pick it.
- Keep any window on top of the others (Ctrl + Win + T).
- Awake and Pick a colour can go on the quick menu and the quick wheel.

Settings and help
- Save your settings to a file and bring them back later. A copy is also made before every update.
- Settings → Shortcuts shows every shortcut in one place, whether it's working, and lets you change it.
- Report a problem (in About) makes a zip of the logs on your Desktop and opens a ready-to-send report. Nothing is sent by itself.

Languages
- A language button at the top of the window changes the language straight away, with no restart.
- On the first start, Corefold asks which language you want.
- New, cleaner dropdown lists.

Corefold Capture
- Record the PC's sound too, not just the microphone: videos, games and calls.
- Pause a recording and carry on. The parts are joined into one video.
- Screenshot with a timer, for menus that close when you click away.
- Pin a screenshot or a copied picture on top of everything while you work (P). Drag to move, wheel to resize, Ctrl + wheel to see through it.
- Numbered steps in the editor, for showing how to do something.
- Record a GIF.

Corefold Clipboard
- Edit a copy before you paste it, or change it in one click: UPPERCASE, lowercase, Capital Letters, remove extra spaces, join into one line, tidy JSON.
- Copy the text inside a copied picture.
- Ctrl + click several texts to paste them together.
- Saved texts: keep an address or a reply you use often pinned at the top, ready to paste.
- Never keep copies from apps you choose, like your bank's app.

Corefold Viewer
- Quick look: select a file in File Explorer or on the desktop and press Ctrl + Space to preview it. Pictures, videos, music, PDFs, text, folders and archives.
- Brightness and colour (A), with Auto.
- Compare a picture with the next one side by side (X).
- Opens camera RAW files, SVG drawings and TIFFs.
- Rename (F2), move (M) or copy a picture to another folder.
- Flip left to right (H) or upside down (V).
- Sort by newest, oldest or biggest.
- A strip of small pictures along the bottom (T shows or hides it).

Corefold Player
- Playlist (Q), repeat and shuffle.
- Chapters on the seek bar and in the menu (Ctrl + Left / Right).
- Play a link (Ctrl + U).
- Fit the picture to the window, fill it, or stretch it (Z).

Converter
- Videos are made with the graphics card (NVIDIA, Intel or AMD) when there is one, so they finish much sooner.
- Crop a video: remove black bars, or make it square, vertical (9:16 for Reels and TikTok) or wide.
- Put subtitles into a video, drawn into the picture or as subtitles you can turn off. Save a video's own subtitles as .srt.
- Save the sound of a video as M4A, MP3, WAV or FLAC.
- Join several videos, or several sound files, into one.
- Several photos are converted at once, and big photos save faster as PNG and JPG.
- Stopping a conversion leaves no half-made file behind.

Corefold Archive
- A preview beside the list: pictures and text, without unpacking (P).
- Make TAR.GZ, TAR.XZ and TAR.ZST archives, and open .tar.zst and .zst files.
- Remember archive passwords, kept locked on this PC. Forget them all from Settings.
- Copy any file's SHA-256 fingerprint to check a download.
- A big archive says it's opening instead of looking stuck.

Corefold Space
- A cleaner, roomier look. Each drive has a ring that shows how full it is, and turns amber, then red, as it fills.
- A path above the map you can click to go back up, and each top folder has its own colour on the map.
- A bar under the map splits the drive by kind of file. Pick a kind and the rest of the map steps back.
- While Space looks through a drive, a ring shows how much it has found so far.
- A drive's map is kept, so opening it again is instant. Drive cards say when a drive was mapped, and Look again maps it afresh.
- Reads more folders at once, so maps finish sooner. Stop answers right away, even in a huge folder.
- What grew since the last look.
- Big files you haven't opened in a year.
- Click a kind (videos, pictures…) to see its biggest files across the drive.
- Clean up more: Windows Update downloads, the previous Windows, caches of Discord, Teams, Slack, WhatsApp and other apps, picture previews, and graphics card caches.
- Finding duplicates is faster.
- Uninstall a program straight from its folder in the map.
- Find empty folders and move them to the Recycle Bin. Windows' and programs' own folders are never touched.

Uninstaller
- See what an app's uninstaller is doing: its window is open, it's working in the background, or it looks stuck, with the time so far. Show its window or stop it.
- If an app wasn't uninstalled, Corefold says why right away instead of waiting.
- Choose how deep to clean up afterwards: Safe, Normal or Deep.
- Finds more leftovers: services, scheduled tasks, file types, taskbar pins and the app's entries in Windows.
- Programs still running are closed first, and files in use are removed when you restart.
- Browser extensions in Chrome, Edge, Brave, Vivaldi, Opera and Firefox, with the ones that can see every website marked.
- Drag onto any window to find the program it belongs to.
- A filter for the extras that came with Windows.
- Right-click a shortcut and choose Uninstall with Corefold.
- The list of apps shows at once, and working out sizes no longer slows the window.
- Removing leftovers shows which item it's on.

Lighter and faster
- Uses less of your PC while it sits in the tray. The quick menu, quick wheel and clipboard popup let go of their memory a minute after they close.
- The Shift-drag wheel checks the mouse less often, and stops completely while the Converter is paused or removed.
- The window and the wheel open faster the first time.

Arabic
- Wrapped lines no longer cut off their last word.
- The right form of a word after a number.

Fixes
- Corefold no longer crashes after adding and removing tools in the Store a few times.

Safety
- Corefold only starts 7-Zip, FFmpeg and Windows' own tools from where they're installed, never a file with the same name lying in the folder you're working in.
- The Uninstaller's administrator steps can't be tricked by an app's name, a leftover's name or a link inside a folder it cleans.

Updating
- Copies older than 1.7.0 can update themselves again, straight to this version.

Coming soon
- Corefold Cloud, and more tools waiting in the Store.

Your settings, history and backups are kept.
