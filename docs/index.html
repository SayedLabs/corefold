How we build

A few promises we keep in every app.

"Rakiza" means a pillar: the thing you lean on without thinking about it. That is the bar we hold our software to.

Your files stay yours

Everything happens on your PC. No accounts, no ads, no tracking, and no server of ours that could collect anything.

Light by design

Our apps wait quietly and hand memory back to Windows. A tool you switch off isn't loaded at all.

Arabic and English, done properly

Right-to-left that feels native, not flipped. Rakiza Core speaks five languages.

Careful with what matters

Originals are never changed. What you remove goes to the Recycle Bin or a backup you can put back.

Privacy

We have no server that could collect your data. On purpose.

Our apps only go online to check for their own updates, and to the cloud accounts you choose to sign in to. Everything else stays on your PC.
