Privacy

Last changed: October 22, 2026

Corefold is a Windows app made by Atomic Works. In short: your files never leave your PC, there are no accounts, no ads and no tracking. The only time Corefold goes online by itself is to check for its own updates.

Your files

Everything Corefold does to your files (converting, shrinking, removing backgrounds, reading text, opening archives, uninstalling apps) happens on your own PC. Nothing is uploaded, to us or to anyone else.

What goes over the internet

WhenWhat is sentTo whom
Checking for updates (a few times a day, can be turned off in Updates) A request for the latest release, with the app's name and version. Like any web request, it carries your IP address. GitHub, which hosts Corefold's releases (GitHub's privacy statement)
Downloading an update, when you choose to or turn on automatic updates A download of the installer.GitHub
When you click Send feedback, Website or similar links Your web browser opens that page. Anything you then write in a feedback form is public on GitHub.GitHub

Corefold doesn't send usage statistics, crash reports or any information about your files. If a future version offers to send a crash report, it will ask you first and show you what would be sent.

What stays on your PC

Corefold keeps its settings, a list of recent conversions and the backups made when you uninstall apps in your user folders (%APPDATA%\Corefold and %LOCALAPPDATA%\Corefold). You can clear the history in the app, and uninstalling Corefold offers to delete all of it.

This website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may keep logs of visits (like your IP address) for security. The site itself has no cookies, analytics or trackers, and loads nothing from other companies.

Children

Corefold doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be on this page with a new date. Questions? Open an issue on GitHub.
